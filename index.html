About

I’m a visual designer and product thinker who blends empathy, strategy, and craft to create digital experiences people love to use. With over 7 years of working experience in branding, UI/UX, and digital design and a previous life as an English teacher. I know how to relate, communicate, and make things complex seem simple. From elegant mobile apps to full design systems, I combine strategy and creativity, starting from empathy and ending with something that is effortless, intuitive, and just the right amount of stunning. My goal? Designs that work hard, look great, and put smiles on people's faces.
