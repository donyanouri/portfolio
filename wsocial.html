W Social

A social platform designed around trust, connection, and human conversation

W Social

W Social is a European social media platform designed to make online conversation feel more open, human, and trustworthy. The experience brings together a clear editorial interface, accessible interactions, and a visual language that feels optimistic without becoming noisy.

As the visual designer and community lead, I translated the platform’s values into a recognizable public presence. I balanced the needs of a growing network with a confident, welcoming first impression.

What I worked on

  • Community growthManaged and developed digital communities across social channels. I created a consistent rhythm of useful, engaging, and conversation-led content.
  • Content directionPlanned content themes, wrote platform copy, and shaped visual storytelling to make W Social’s purpose easy to understand and inviting to new audiences.
  • Brand identityDesigned logos, banners, social media graphics, campaign visuals, and marketing materials to build recognition across every touchpoint.
  • Visual systemsCreated reusable templates and layout rules so the team could produce content quickly while keeping the brand coherent, expressive, and unmistakably W Social.
  • Audience communicationAdapted messages for different formats and communities. I responded to feedback and turned audience insight into clearer communication.
