DEI-focused recruitment research & toolkit

Tobii Dynavox

Collaborated with a cross-functional team to research and reimagine Tobii Dynavox's recruitment process from a Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) standpoint.

Conducted 23 qualitative interviews and reviewed survey results to identify systemic recruitment obstacles. Developed inclusive candidate personas, user journeys, and a DEI Hiring Toolkit with accessible job description templates, interview formats, and evaluation guidelines. Provided a measurable DEI roadmap with KPIs, offering actionable recommendations to stakeholders. Gave a human-centric, scalable solution that maximizes the accessibility, equity, and inclusion of recruitment practice.
