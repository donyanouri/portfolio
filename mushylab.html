Mushylab

I designed a digital platform that allows users to create personalized mushroom supplement blends. The project enables people to explore different functional mushrooms, understand their benefits, and combine them based on goals such as focus, calm, immunity, and energy. The platform emphasizes transparency, customization, and a clear user journey from mushroom selection to final personalized blend.
